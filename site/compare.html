STRAIGHT TALK

When something else fits better.

  • Proposal drafting, CRM, pipeline management, or bid scoring: the paid platforms above do this; 1102tools does not.
  • State and local opportunities: 1102tools covers federal sources only.
  • One install for dozens of sources: broad local packages such as cliwant/mcp-sam-gov cover more sources, with fewer tools per source.

How this was researched

Researched September 24, 2026 in the Claude connector directory, the official MCP Registry, GitHub, Smithery, PulseMCP, and Glama. Prices come from each vendor's public pricing page. The ChatGPT app directory could not be searched automatically. Competitor tool counts come from their public listings; competitor software and tests were not run.

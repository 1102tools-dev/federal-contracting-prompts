PRIVACY

Built to collect
as little as possible.

1102tools is built and run by one person. It's free and it isn't a business. There's nothing to sell, so there's no reason to track you.

YOUR CHOICE

Want nothing to pass through 1102tools at all? Use Local. Each server is a small program on your own computer that connects straight to the government sites. It isn't an AI model, so it doesn't need a powerful computer. If your computer runs the Claude or ChatGPT desktop app, it can run Local. Your AI app can set it up for you.

WHAT STAYS TRUE

What 1102tools does and doesn't keep.

  • No accounts, cookies, ads or tracking. The only analytics is Cloudflare's visit count, which uses no cookies and no IP addresses.
  • 1102tools doesn't log or keep IP addresses.
  • Your searches aren't logged or saved, and nobody reads them.
  • Government sites see requests from 1102tools, not from you.
  • Some public government answers are kept for a limited time so repeat questions come back faster and agencies aren't asked the same thing twice. The cache holds the public answer, not who asked.
  • The only record is a short service record for each call (which tool, which app, whether it worked, whether it found results, found nothing or hit an error, how long it took), kept to keep the servers running. It has no search terms, results or IP addresses.
  • Your AI app (Claude, ChatGPT or another) handles your conversation under its own privacy policy.
  • CUI (Controlled Unclassified Information): CUI is a question for your AI app, not for 1102tools. The servers only look up public government data. Your chat, your files and anything you paste stay with your AI app, so whether CUI can go in your chat depends on that app and your agency's or company's rules. This is the same for Local and Hosted. On hosted, 1102tools gets only the search your AI sends, like a NAICS code or a vendor name, and doesn't log it.

Each server's notice

Cache times and data handling differ a little by server. Each hosted server publishes its own notice, and that notice is the final word for that server.
